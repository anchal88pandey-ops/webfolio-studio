Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You share your information and preferences, and I handle the design and development.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every package is designed to be responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

How does payment work?

The price is the package fee shown above. Payment is completed at the final approval stage before website handover, and the payment method and any other details are confirmed with you in writing before work begins.

Will I own my website?

Yes. Your content belongs to you, and the finished website files are handed over to you after final approval and payment.

How long does delivery take?

Final delivery timelines are confirmed after I receive your content, because they depend on the package and how quickly information and feedback are shared.
